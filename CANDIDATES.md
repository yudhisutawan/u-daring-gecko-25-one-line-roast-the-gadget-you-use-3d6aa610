# Candidates — One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day

Competition: u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610
Gate: one line, 1–200 characters. All 10 candidates below pass the gate.

Judge criteria (in order, from competition.yaml):
1. Roasts a gadget/app people use every day in a single line (names or clearly targets it).
2. Actually funny: lands on first read, sting of recognition, not a mild grumble.
3. Fresh: angle/comparison/detail not seen before, not the obvious complaint.
4. Neatly worded: tight, well timed, no wasted words, inside the limit.
5. One person's specific gripe, concrete detail about how the thing behaves, not generic.

Deliberately avoided: the brief's own examples (phone dies at 4pm, app asks for
a rating before it opens, smart kettle firmware update) — those will be the most
copied angles in the field, so they lose on criterion 3 before judging starts.
